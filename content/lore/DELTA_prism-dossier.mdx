### [Codename] DOSSIER

**Classification:** DELTA  
**Filed:** 2025-04-23

## Summary
It appears this was necessary to put in here. Or so I've been told. Not my problem what stays and what goes.

## Description
Smol fluffy fox-like creature. Approximately two feet in height. Weightless as far as I can tell, for they has no effect on a scale, like holding nothing when lifting, yet clearly solid mass, tested by placing blanket over it. Rainbow coloured, but without consistency. Their colours seem to follow the angle you are viewing. Except their eyes are dark, yet glitter like a starry sky.

### Containment Procedures
A regular supply of milk to drink, positive social interaction, and minimum of 10 headpats a day keeps them tame and comfy. Under emergency circumstances, can be contained in a pitch black airtight space. It can be done without worry, for they does not breath, produce bodily waste, seem to get bored with surroundings, or even age as far as observed. So long as they're brought extra effort towards their comfort upon release.

### Incident Logs
Informal log, reason: laziness
First contact, dated 01/24/2007
This creature first appeared in amidst snowstorm near the Alaskan/Canadian border, reported by children who assumed it a dream the following day. The creature producing a lightshow amidst the snow, one child relating it to the northern lights, another claiming a rave party, the last description saying every snowflake appearing coloured. The only report on record, until appearance near containment facility roughly three years ago.

### Analysis
The creature does appear harmless, though too mystical and mysterious to trust in regular civilization until further notice. Easy to scare though if not provided with comfort from day to day.
