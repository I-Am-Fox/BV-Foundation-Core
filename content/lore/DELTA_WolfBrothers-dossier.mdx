### ASSET-DE001 'Wolf Brothers' DOSSIER

**Classification:** DELTA CLASS
**Filed:** 2019-07-15

# ███ SECURE OPERATIONS FILE – ASSET DOCKET

**Clearance Level Required:** Level 5: ████████████ & TRAINED PSYCHIATRISTS ONLY

## Summary
ASSET-DE001 ‘Wolf Brothers’ designates a pair of genetically altered humanoid entities originating from Canis lupus. While DE001 and DE001-2 demonstrate a bonded relationship, it is DE001 that exhibits aggressive tendencies. This subject shows a severe memetic-reality distortion effect, specifically affecting documentation, memory, and verbal recognition of its capabilities. Efforts to document, transcribe, or analyze DE001 have consistently resulted in cognitive and physical deterioration of the observer.

Reports from all interacting personnel consistently characterize DE001 as "cruel, but only when necessary," with its aggression often triggered by attention directed toward DE001-2. Restoration efforts for altered subjects have been unsuccessful, with all attempts exacerbating the anomalous effects.

Analysis by Black Veil indicates that the observed reality distortions are not merely defensive, but rather intentionally shaped, suggesting DE001 actively manages its own mythos and public perception. The underlying motivations remain undetermined.

## Description
ASSET-DE001 is a designation for two humanoid organisms mutated from a sub-species of the common Gray Wolf (Canis Lupus) (DE001 & DE001-2). It is noted that one is larger than the other and is the primary source of any violence.

DE001 is capable of altering reality pertaining to any known information about them, it will also modify any and all reality to brutally ██████ or in some cases ██████ those who create and documentation about their abilities.

Victims altered by DE001, when asked or prompted during interviews or interrogations regale DE001 as "cruel, but only when absolutely necessary". It is also noted that the source of the 'cruelty' stems from any attempts to interact with DE001-2.

All attempts to restore altered subjects have only led to further alteration. Though the exact nature of said alteration is unclear despite rigorous and exhaustive investigations by Dr. ███████ & Black Veil ██████████████ Teams.

### Containment Procedures
DE001 & DE001-2 are to be contained within a 5m x 5m x 5m containment chamber, walls are to be at no less than 25cm thick. Ample bedding is required for both DE001 & DE001-2. Any other additional items that are to be introduced into the containment chamber are subject to authorization from Overseer ████.

Personnel are instructed to only interact with DE-001, this includes but is in no means an exhaustive list;
• Calling their name - ███████
• Making eye contact with DE001-2
• Any and all attempts to touch DE001-2

### Incident Logs
Log DE-A (06/04/25):

███████ was instructed to enter the containment chamber.

Shortly after ███████ entered the containment chamber security footage became unreliable and corrupted beyond a usable scope.

This was however contrary to the already substantiated and believed abilities of DE-001.

Aside from unusable recorded footage, audio remains intact and has been investigated thoroughly. Quiet whispers of 'This is what happens when you get too close' were heard inlaid within the audio recording. It is noted however that personnel present at the time have no recollection of these words being spoken.

███████ was no where to be seen within the containment chamber after the recording was reinstated. A mere 15 minutes had passed since ███████'s introduction to the containment chamber.

### Analysis
It is unclear at present the full abilities and motive of DE001 and by extension DE001-2.
