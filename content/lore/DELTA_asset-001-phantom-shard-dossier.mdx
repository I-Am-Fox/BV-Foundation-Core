### [“Phantom] DOSSIER

**Classification:** DELTA  
**Filed:** 2025-04-23

## Summary
A translucent shard that phases through solid matter unpredictably.

## Description
Recovered from the ruins of Site-12 after a containment breach. The Phantom Shard exhibits quantum tunneling at macro scales. Test logs show it reappears at random intervals up to 50 meters away.

### Containment Procedures
- Keep in a reinforced lead-lined chamber.
- Monitor with phase-detection sensors.
- Two security personnel on standby at all times.

### Incident Logs
- Log 07-02: Shard disappeared during transfer.
- Log 07-03: Detected inside secure storage vault wall.
- Log 07-10: Participant Q attempted unauthorized retrieval.

### Analysis
The Phantom Shard appears to violate conservation of mass locally. Recommend accelerated research into phase-stabilization fields.
